Graduate Research Assistant
Jul. 2023 – present
Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory (PPPL) · Advisor: Prof. Hantao Ji
  • Explained an 18-year-old puzzle: MRX electron current sheets are 3–5× thicker than kinetic simulations predicted. Developed cylindrical semi-collisional PIC simulations at realistic mass ratio and an analytical electron-orbit model that reproduce the measured width (~6–7 electron skin depths).
  • AI for magnetic reconnection (DOE Genesis Mission, with NVIDIA): designed the plasmoid-detection pipeline (magnetic-probe array + fast camera; plasmoid count, location, size) and a three-tier simulation training strategy; generated ~1,080 VPIC training runs.
  • Ported a cylindrical particle-in-cell (PIC) code to VPIC 2.0 (C++/Kokkos) for GPU/CPU and validated it against the legacy code; one GPU node delivers 15–20× more particle pushes per hour than one CPU node.
  • Built and verified a kinetic electron–neutral collision operator in VPIC (elastic, excitation, ionization).
  • Built a planar laser-induced fluorescence diagnostic that turns Doppler-resolved camera images into 2D, phase-resolved ion velocity distributions; revealed radial ion outflow and anomalous ion heating in an electron-beam-generated E×B plasma.
  • In-situ diagnostic of channel erosion in Hall thrusters
  • Investigation of anomalous ion plume divergence in Hall thrusters
Graduate Research Assistant
Sep. 2020 – Jun. 2023
Seoul National University
  • Quantified how plasma-parameter uniformity in a CCP etch source drives etch uniformity; built a controller that tunes plasma parameters to set the etch result.
  • Built a virtual-metrology model that predicts plasma density distribution in capacitively coupled Ar plasma from measured plasma variables.
  • Showed with experiments and 2D PIC that plasma leaking into the gas-injection gaps of an ICP etch source pulls breakdown voltage below the Paschen curve, and that wall erosion lowers it further.
